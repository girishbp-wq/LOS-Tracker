# LOS Tracker
   👉 **[Open LOS Tracker](https://girishbp-wq.github.io/LOS-Tracker/)**
   Professional dashboard for tracking Line of Sponsorship and business metrics across months.
   
   ## Features
   - 📊 Upload monthly LOS files (Excel)
   - 🌳 Hierarchical tree view with expand/collapse
   - 📈 Multi-month comparison
   - 👤 Individual ABO scorecards
   - 📋 Team rollups and month-over-month analysis
   - 💾 Save/load workbooks
   
   ## How to Use
   1. Click "Upload" tab
   2. Drop your monthly LOS Excel files
   3. Click "📊 Dashboard" to explore
   4. Use other tabs for detailed analysis
   
   **All data stays in your browser - nothing is uploaded to a server.**
